Black Screen Online — Minimal Dark Display & Screen Utility

Black Screen Online is an online full-screen tool designed to provide a completely clean display canvas. It works across desktops, laptops, tablets, and smartphones without installing external software.

Core Features

  • True Black Screen: Ideal for OLED/AMOLED power saving, dead pixel testing, and screen cleaning.
  • Utility Screen Colors: Pure black, pure white lightbox, 50% neutral gray for screen uniformity, warm reading light, and RGB dead pixel test colors.
  • Ambient Live Clock: Subtle 12-hour digital clock with dimmed opacity to avoid visual distraction.
  • Custom Focus Text: Add a personalized mantra, focus goal, or meeting note directly onto the screen.
  • Quick Keyboard Shortcuts: Press F for fullscreen, C for the color palette, and Escape to dismiss.
  • Auto-Save Preferences: Automatically saves your selected background color and custom text in local storage.

Common Practical Uses

1. OLED and AMOLED Screen Power Saving

Because OLED screens switch off individual black pixels completely, displaying a pure black screen reduces power consumption and prevents panel burn-in.

2. Cleaning Dust and Smudges on Monitors

A pure black screen illuminated in a well-lit room highlights fingerprint smudges, specks of dust, and grime on monitor surfaces.

3. Dual-Monitor Focus and Sleep

When working on a multi-monitor workstation, Black Screen Online allows you to black out secondary displays to eliminate peripheral distraction.

4. Dead Pixel and Backlight Bleed Testing

Detect stuck pixels or IPS monitor backlight bleed across the entire panel in fullscreen mode.
